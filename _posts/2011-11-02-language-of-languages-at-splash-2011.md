In October 2011, we presented the Language of Languages workbench demo at the SPLASH/OOPSLA 2011 conference in Portland, Oregon.

Our presentation illustrated how LoLs tackles notation consistency through step-by-step demonstrations. We started with a multi-notation calculator (supporting Western numerals, Roman numerals, stack machines, and graphical displays) and extended it to complex state machine models and system architecture workflows.
