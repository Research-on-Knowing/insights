Building on years of fieldwork, public partnership progress, and evolving technology, we have drafted an updated edition of our white paper, [Creating Everyone Everywhere Affordable Broadband Access: Update]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/pdfs/universal-broadband-updated-framework.pdf).

**Key highlights of the updated paper**

- **Post-pandemic reality:** The COVID-19 pandemic shifted broadband from a convenience to a critical utility. The move to remote work and digital health care makes symmetrical fiber essential.
- **Whatcom County case study:** An analysis of the successes and challenges of the Port of Bellingham and Whatcom PUD #1 deployments, including grant wins in East Nooksack, North Lynden, and North Ferndale, and lessons from cost and permitting delays in Point Roberts.
- **TCO and construction trade-offs:** A detailed look at total cost of ownership (TCO) trade-offs between aerial and buried fiber, pole replacement fees, and long-term network maintenance.
- **Commercial acceleration:** How municipal open-access builds have encouraged private ISPs, such as Ziply Fiber, to accelerate capital deployment in smaller communities like Lynden, Ferndale, Everson, and Maple Falls.

**Next steps**

- **Search for commercial partners:** Look for a commercial company interested in building and operating a wholesale fiber network.
- **Encourage local broadband activists:** Recruit community members to become local neighborhood advocates.
