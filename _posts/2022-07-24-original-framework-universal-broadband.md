To translate the principles of public fiber into an actionable roadmap for local leaders, we published our foundational framework, [Creating Everyone Everywhere Affordable Broadband Access]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/pdfs/universal-broadband-original-framework.pdf).

**Key principles of the framework**

- **Symmetrical bandwidth standards:** Essential service is defined as at least 100 Mbps symmetrical (download and upload) for $70 a month, scaling to 1 Gbps symmetrical for $100 a month.
- **Network decomposition:** Large municipal builds are broken into manageable, neighborhood-scale projects, such as Local Utility Districts (LUDs) and “Fiberhoods.”
- **Public open-access architecture:** Rural market failures are resolved by treating the link-layer fiber grid like a public road network.
- **Dig Once mandates:** Fiber conduit is installed whenever roads, water lines, or utilities are opened or repaired, which lowers deployment costs.

This framework gave community members, local port commissioners, and regional planning groups a blueprint for municipal broadband expansion.
